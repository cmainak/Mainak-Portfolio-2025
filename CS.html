Honoring heritage through modern corporate design.

A comprehensive brand identity project featuring logo design, color theory, and packaging mockups for a family run soda company gearing up for a national launch.

01. The Background

Candle Soda is a 75 year-old, family run soda company known for its natural probiotic drinks. As it gears up for a national launch, the brand is looking to revamp its logo something more modern that still honors its long-standing heritage and resonates with today’s health-conscious audience.

Brand Parameters

01 Flavors Cola, Lemon Lime, Orange, Grapes, Root Beer
02 Price Point $2 per 20oz Bottle / $6 per Six-Pack
03 Keywords Natural, Healthy, Flavorful
