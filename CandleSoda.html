Background

Candle Soda, founded in 1949 in Chicago, Illinois, is a 75-year-old family-owned beverage company that crafts natural, health-focused drinks. As it prepares for a nationwide launch, the brand is in need of a revamp — its current identity feels outdated and makes it harder to connect with newer audiences.
