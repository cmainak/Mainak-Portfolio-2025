GBLL BRAND SYSTEM

Building a flexible visual identity & style guide from the ground up. Green’s Blue Light Lounge set out to reimagine the Upper West Side bar and dining experience by blending the sophistication of a modern cocktail lounge with the warmth of a neighborhood restaurant. I designed a comprehensive brand system and visual style guide to ensure visual consistency across print menus, signage, marketing touchpoints, and digital interfaces.

ROLE Lead Visual & Graphic Designer
TIMELINE May 2024
TOOLS Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop
DOCUMENT Download Full PDF ↗

The Context

A high-end restaurant and lounge requires an identity that scales gracefully across contrasting environments—from low-lit ambient interior menus to bright outdoor billboards and small mobile screens. Without strict clear-space metrics, locked typographic hierarchies, and cross-media color standards (CMYK vs RGB), visual brands quickly become diluted when handed off to external vendors or production teams.

The Response

I established a robust, scalable brand architecture enforcing strict vector precision, exact color matching, and a flexible collateral ecosystem. This included clear-space parameters and a typographic hierarchy that balances a moody, vibrant lounge aesthetic to prevent accidental distortion during fast production cycles.

Identified Design Objectives

01 Vector Precision & Safety Define clear-space boundaries and usage prohibitions to guard logo integrity.
02 Cross-Media Color Standards Document exact HEX, RGB, and CMYK color formulas for print and digital publishing.
03 Collateral Ecosystem Apply brand rules to menus, stationery, outdoor ads, dinnerware, and app screens.

Logo Architecture & Rules

To maintain logomark clarity against dark interior backgrounds and bright digital displays, I established a strict clear-space metric based on the letter height 'B'. No text or visual graphics may invade this perimeter.

Brand Governance (Do's & Don'ts)

"Locking down core logo parameters prevents accidental distortion during fast production cycles."

Logomark Do's:
  • Scale proportionally at all times.
  • Use official color formulas on approved backdrops.
Prohibited Misuses:
  • Do not squish, stretch, or alter logo proportions.
  • Do not swap fonts or change logomark placement.

Color & Typography System

To give the lounge its signature nighttime look, I paired moody blacks and deep greens with a vibrant neon green accent (#9CF400). Headlines use Gagalin for bold presence, while custom script accents (Brush Script MT) add warmth to dining menus.

Real-World Applications

Stationery & Business Collateral

Corporate envelopes, business cards, and official letterhead designs feature dark green header bands and high-contrast logo placements.

Food & Beverage Menus

Multi-column menu spreads designed with clean typographic spacing ensure fast reading under warm, low-light lounge lighting.

Large-Format Billboard & Merchandise

High-impact billboard layouts and branded apparel showcase clear visual hierarchy optimized for fast recognition from a distance.

Digital App Interface

I translated the visual guidelines into a mobile application UI, extending the brand to online reservations, digital waitlists, and interactive food ordering.

Reflect

Designing the GBLL brand identity highlighted the importance of clear governance when producing collateral across print, merchandise, outdoor advertising, and mobile screens.

By standardizing color formulas, clear-space boundaries, and typographic rules early, production teams can build marketing and pitch materials efficiently while maintaining brand integrity.
