HABIT BOOST

Imagine making a promise to yourself...

To cook more, start a fitness routine, learn a new language, pick up a new craft, or even to drink more water... The first week goes well with meeting your goals. The second week, not so much. Then eventually you lose all hope with your new commitment.

THE PROBLEM

How can we boost user motivation to maintain positive habits?

People often fail to maintain creating long term positive habits due to a lack of personalization and understanding of user psychology, especially at low motivational points.

Research

RESEARCHING PROBLEM SPACE

Identifying 'Hook' model for habit formation

In a literature review of habit frameworks, my research uncovered a particularly effective approach called the Hook Model. This model aids in increasing people's investment in habit formation by using a cyclical process of triggers, actions, rewards, and investments.

ANALYZING MARKET & COMPETITORS

Confirming identical feature sets in over-saturated market

The marketplace is saturated with apps focused on habit formation. Our first step was to identify the popular players within the space and understand the main feature sets.

We discovered that the features across the apps was centered around Goal Tracking. We hypothesized that this is key in habit forming behaviour and there is an opportunity to position ourselves differently through our approach.

SUS SCORE

Refocusing and reorganizing hierarchy

1. Removing Bottom Navigation

Introducing additional primary navigation distracted users from the main app functions. We relegated secondary functions to be less prominent.

2. Bringing actions closer

Hiding the Habits screen behind Progress makes elements separate. Putting them on the same page allows for instant gratification.

3. A clearer focus on my habits

Although people enjoy seeing their progress, overview is still the first sought information.

Reflections

CHALLENGES & LEARNINGS

1. Simplicity vs. Personalization: Initial designs overwhelmed users with customization options. Solution: Implemented "smart defaults" with progressive disclosure users start simple, customize later. Reduced onboarding from 3+ min to 45 sec.

2. Accessibility Oversight: 3 colorblind users couldn't distinguish habit status by color alone. Redesigned using icons + color + text labels, achieving WCAG AA compliance and improving clarity for all users.

3. Late-Stage IA Testing: Discovered navigation confusion in week 5, requiring significant rework. Learning: Test information architecture early with lo-fi prototypes, not after investing in high-fidelity designs.

4. Feature Bloat: Removed social features after testing showed 78% of users preferred private tracking. Result: User satisfaction increased from 72% to 92%. Learning: The best feature is sometimes the one you don't build.
