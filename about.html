A Little About Me

Hello, I'm Mainak! (MY-naak)

My journey into UXD was not linear. I started my career in Media, working for production houses and ad agencies. That experience gave me a sharp eye for visual storytelling and content creation.

Unlike many designers, I also worked on the research side, specifically on consumer research and product testing studies. work that revealed the gap between user desires and actual needs. This research experience pushed me toward advanced design studies and taught me the ability to see the complete picture and execute on it. Now I don't just understand users I translate that insights into user-centerd design.

Right now, I’m focused on the intersection of Design and AI. I am actively learning about Adaptive/Responsive UI, Design Systems, to build ethical, accessible products.

I am a Manchester United fan, and if you are one too, let's believe in better days…
