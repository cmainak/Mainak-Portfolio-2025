A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Mainak! (MY-nuhk)

I'm a Product Designer who blends research, design, storytelling, and a bit of code to build products that actually work for people. In my most recent experience, I genuinely enjoy conducting user testing, running product tests, and prototype new solutions for interface redesigns. My path to Product design wasn't linear, but it was very intentional. I started my career in creative roles, working for production houses and creative agencies, which gave me a sharp eye for visual storytelling and branding.

Later I moved into market research at Ipsos, where I worked on the quantitative research side, specifically on product testing, competitive benchmarking and brand-health tracking studies, coordinated the end to end execution, from initial methodology planning and study design to the final execution and delivery. While this taught me how to listen to users through data at scale, I found myself constantly wondering how those data points translated into the actual interfaces people interact with every day.

However, it made me want to explore the design world more in-depth from research to final design, that drive lead me to pursue my Master's in UX/UI & Interaction Design at Touro, where I deepened my knowledge in design thinking, user-centered design principles, interaction patterns, learned to code, and built this site myself from scratch.

I thrive on turning complex problems into seamless, intuitive experiences because great design should feel as effortless as a perfect cover drive in cricket (yes, I'm a huge sports fan too!). Right now, I'm focused on the intersection of design and AI. I'm actively learning about adaptive responsive UI and design systems to build ethical, accessible products.
