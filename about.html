A Little About Me

Hello, I'm Mainak! (MY-nuk)

Born and raised in Mumbai, India. I began my career in creative roles, working in multimedia design and marketing, where I learned content creation, creative direction, Visual Design, and managing campaigns.

My shift to UX began while I was working at a research organization. Over the course of a consumer research and product testing projects and studies, I gained hands-on experience designing surveys, by scripting surveys, cleaning data, coding open-ended responses, performing basic analysis, and preparing reports to ensure accuracy and clarity in research findings.

Through that work, I became deeply curious about not just what users want, but how those consumer insights could directly shape better experiences and product decisions. That curiosity is what inspired me to pursue advanced studies in design. So I could understand the design side of the process and apply research across the full product journey, from discovery and user needs to prototyping and testing.

Outside of work, I love exploring new AI tools that can help teams move faster and think deeper. I’m also actively learning about AI ethics, accessibility best practices, adaptive and responsive design across devices, and emerging concepts like Agentic AI and advanced prompting areas I see as essential for building responsible, inclusive products in the years ahead.
