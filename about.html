In a nutshell...

My interest in design began with a simple obsession: I could not stop dissecting the apps I used daily. Whether it was a music player or a fitness tracker, I was constantly analyzing the user experience, questioning the usability heuristics, and identifying specific friction points that either facilitated or blocked user goals.

That curiosity evolved into a professional career. Over the last three years, I have moved from analyzing applications to designing & developing them. I enjoy the full end-to-end product lifecycle, starting from the initial discovery and strategy phase to high-fidelity implementation and design handoff.

Whether I'm designing a new interface, prototyping interactions, or writing code, I approach every project with enthusiasm and attention to detail. I'm constantly learning and evolving my skills to stay at the forefront of design and technology.

Outside of design, I’m a big sports fan. I play football and basketball whenever I get the chance. My downtime is spent reading voraciously, hunting for new music, and traveling.
