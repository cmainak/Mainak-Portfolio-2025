A Little About Me

Hello, I'm Mainak! (MY-nuk)

I grew up in Mumbai, India and completed my undergrad in Mass Media. After graduation, I worked at a few companies exploring design, strategy, and consumer insights.

My UX journey started while I was working at a market research and consumer insights organization.Over the course of a few projects and studies, I got hands-on experience understanding how people interacted with different products and campaigns—and noticed that no strategy is foolproof; research insights can always surprise you if we overlook the user’s perspective. That’s when it clicked: understanding the user deeply had to be at the heart of any product design.

That curiosity for understanding challenges and exploring creative solutions naturally led me to UX and interaction design, where I could merge my research background with problem-solving, creativity, and technology.
