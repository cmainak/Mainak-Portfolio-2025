A Little About Me

Hi, I’m Mainak Chakraborty!

As a human-centred practitioner, I place the user at the heart of every design decision. My strenghts lies in uncovering user pain points through mixed-method research and collaborating closely with product and engineering teams to build solutions that are both efficient and strategic delivered in agile, iterative cycles.

Over the past few years, I’ve worked across media, marketing, market research, UI design, and front-end design. This mix has shaped me approach product design from multiple angles not just as visual problem-solving, but as a balance between user value, business goals, and execution. I approach every challenge with curiosity, empathy, and a drive to turn insights into meaningful action.

Outside of design, I unwind through getting behind the lens, hitting the trails, or kicking around a football (and cheering on my favorite teams too)

Feel free to reach out. I am always open for collaboration or conversations.
