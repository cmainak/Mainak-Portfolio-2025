A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Mainak! (MY-nuhk)

I'm a UX/UI designer who blends research, design, and a bit of code to build products that actually work for people. In my most recent experience, I worked as an AI designer, and I genuinely enjoy conducting user testing, running product tests, analyzing streaming data, and interface redesigns.

My journey into UXD wasn't linear. I started my career in media, working for production houses and ad agencies, which gave me a sharp eye for visual storytelling and content creation. I then shifted into research at Ipsos, where I worked on the quantitative research side, specifically understanding user preferences studies, running product tests, analyzing streaming data. That research experience taught me how valuable data is when making design decisions. It also made me want to explore the design world more in-depth from research to final design, which inspired me to pursue a Master's in UX/UI & Interaction Design at Touro in Fall 2024, where I deepened my understanding of design thinking, user-centered design principles, interaction patterns, learned to code, and built this portfolio site myself from scratch.

I thrive on turning complex problems into seamless, intuitive experiences because great design should feel as effortless as a perfect cover drive in cricket (yes, I'm a huge sports fan too!). Right now, I'm focused on the intersection of design and AI. I'm actively learning about adaptive responsive UI and design systems to build ethical, accessible products.
