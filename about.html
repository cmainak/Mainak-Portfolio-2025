A Little About Me

Hi, I’m Mainak Chakraborty!

As a human-centred practitioner with a background in media, market research and extended experience in user-experience design.

Over the past few years, I’ve worked across media, marketing, market research. This mix has shaped me approach product design from multiple angles not just as visual problem-solving, but as a balance between user value, business goals, and execution.

Working in Market Research taught me both qualitative and quantitative research methods. I got to dive deep into understanding people’s behaviors, crafting survey questions that made sense, and translating all that data into stories that brands could actually use.

What I really took from that experience is the importance of asking the right questions — and knowing that good research is about listening, not assuming. Working in fast-paced projects taught me how to work with tight timelines, collaborate across teams, and manage the little details that can make or break a study.

These skills naturally pushed me toward UX, where I get to bring research and design together to build things that genuinely help people. My background helps me stay curious, think critically, and make sure I’m designing with the actual user in mind — not just what I think they need.

Outside of design, I unwind through getting behind the lens, hitting the trails, or playing football.

Feel free to reach out. I am always open for collaboration or conversations.
