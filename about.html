Hello, I'm Mainak 👋 (MY-nuhk) "A bit about me, and how I got here."

I'm a Product Designer from Mumbai, India. I graduated with a BA in Mass Media from the University of Mumbai in 2019, and went on to complete a Masters in UX/UI and Interaction Design from Touro University, Manhattan, NY. I played Football (Soccer) for my high school team and have stayed connected to sports like Basketball, Cricket, Table Tennis, and Badminton throughout. I am competitive by nature and enjoy picking up new sports and skills whenever I can.

My interest in product design started somewhere less obvious: a habit of obsessively exploring new apps. Music players, sports score trackers, fitness tools, anything that launched and got people talking. I was never just using them. I was pulling them apart, asking why features were built a certain way, what felt intuitive, what felt like friction, and why some apps stuck while others disappeared in a week. That curiosity quietly became a career.

I thoroughly enjoy the full design process, from developing briefs and strategising, through ideation and concept development, all the way to building. Over three years working across product design and consumer research, collaborating with designers, researchers, engineers and stakeholders from various fields, I have come to find deep satisfaction in work driven toward a clear purpose: making things meaningfully better for people. I pride myself on being a solid collaborator.

Outside of work, I am drawn to all kinds of storytelling: movies, books, blogs, vlogs, and comics. I am a voracious reader, an audiophile, a word enthusiast and a travel lover. Exploring new places and observing how different people live, what they value and how they move through the world, is something that keeps my curiosity alive.
