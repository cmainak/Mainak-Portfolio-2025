A Little About Me

Hello, I'm Mainak! (MY-nuk)

Born and raised in Mumbai, India. I began my career in creative roles, working in multimedia design and marketing, where I learned content creation, creative direction, Visual Design, and managing campaigns.

My shift to UX began while I was working at a research organization. Over the course of a consumer research and product testing projects and studies, I gained hands-on experience designing surveys, by scripting surveys, cleaning data, coding open-ended responses, performing basic analysis, and preparing reports to ensure accuracy and clarity in research findings.

That curiosity for understanding challenges and exploring creative solutions naturally led me to UX and interaction design, where I could merge my research background with problem-solving, creativity, and technology.

I am currently expanding my knowledge on topics like A.I ethics, Innovation Strategy and Accessibility which I feel are going to be of high importance in the near future.
