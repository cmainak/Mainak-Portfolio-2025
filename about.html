A Little About Me

Hello, I’m Mainak! (MY-nuk)

I’m a human-centered practitioner with a background in media, advertising and consumer insights, which helps me approach design from multiple perspectives.

In my past roles, I built a foundation in both qualitative and quantitative research, which I now use to inform design decisions. Skilled in design thinking, wireframing, prototyping, and usability testing, I focus on creating web and mobile experiences that are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with business goals.

I thrive in fast-paced projects, collaborating across teams, and translating complex research findings into actionable design solutions. What drives me is asking the right questions and ensuring I design with real users in mind — not assumptions.

Outside of work, you’ll find me gaming, playing football, or exploring the outdoors.
