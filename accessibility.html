I am committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. I am continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to https://mainakc.netlify.app/.

Our Commitment

I believe that the web should be available and accessible to anyone, regardless of ability. I strive to:

  • Provide an inclusive experience for users with visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.
  • Continuously improve the accessibility of my content, design, and code.
  • Conduct periodic reviews and audits of this portfolio website.

Measures I Take

To support accessibility, I have integrated the following practices directly into my design system:

  • Use strict semantic HTML structure to support assistive technologies.
  • Provide highly descriptive alternative text for all meaningful images and case study assets.
  • Maintain WCAG-compliant color contrast between text and background across both Light and Dark themes.
  • Support full keyboard navigation, including visible focus states and skip links.
  • Ensure ARIA landmarks and aria-labels are applied to interactive components.

Conformance Standard

I aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines are built on four core principles, summarized as POUR:

01
Perceivable
Information and interface elements must be presentable to users in ways they can perceive (alt text, captions, sufficient color contrast).
02
Operable
Interface elements must be operable by all users (frictionless keyboard navigation, sufficient time to read content, no seizure-inducing flashes).
03
Understandable
Information and operation of the interface must be understandable (readable text, predictable navigation patterns, clear hierarchy).
04
Robust
Content must be robust enough to be reliably interpreted by a wide variety of user agents and assistive technologies.

Limitations & Feedback

Despite my best efforts, some content on this website may not yet be fully accessible—such as older PDF documents or third-party embeds. If you encounter content on this website that is not accessible to you, please reach out via the contact form on the homepage so I can address the issue promptly.
